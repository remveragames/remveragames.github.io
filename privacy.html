Privacy Policy — Gemstill: Potion Sort

Last updated: DRAFT — set the date when this page goes live.

Gemstill: Potion Sort is a game by Remvera Games. This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, and why.

Information the game itself collects

The game does not ask for your name, email address, or account. Your progress and settings are saved on your own device. Remvera Games does not run its own servers and does not receive your game data.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information, including the Android advertising ID, to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are used, and you can change that choice in the game's privacy options.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases — Google Play

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Remvera Games does not see or store your payment card details. Google Play's handling of purchase information is covered by Google's privacy policy, above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@gemstill.com
